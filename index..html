About KiTools.in

KiTools is built and maintained by the KiTools Team — a small group based in India that got tired of bouncing between five different websites just to calculate GST, check an EMI, or convert a few units. So we built one place that does it all, properly, without forcing a login or burying the calculator under three pages of ads before you can use it.

Every tool on this page runs entirely in your browser — your numbers, salary details, and personal inputs are never sent to our servers or stored anywhere. The five tools marked AI-Powered are the exception: those send only the specific text you type (like a job title or product name) to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a result, and nothing else about you is shared.

We keep the lights on through affiliate partnerships — when you click through to a card, loan, or shopping deal in our Deals & Offers section and complete a purchase or application, we may earn a small commission. It never costs you anything extra, and it's how a free tool site like this stays free. We only list partners we've personally checked are live and working.
